California registration. In California, businesses that repair major home appliances for a fee must register with the Bureau of Household Goods and Services (BHGS) as service dealers. You can look a company up in the Department of Consumer Affairs license search. If a shop published a registration number on its own website, we record that as the shop's claim — we do not invent license numbers, and you should still confirm that a registration is current before you book. From the business

  • Izzy 5 Star, LLC says it provides appliance diagnostics, repairs, and installation for homeowners across Riverside, San Bernardino, and Los Angeles counties. Its BBB profile lists a Beaumont, CA 92223 address and names Banning, Beaumont, Calimesa, Hemet, Redlands, San Jacinto, and Yucaipa as service areas.
  • Services named on the website: refrigerator and freezer repair, washer and dryer repair, oven, stove, and cooktop repair, dishwasher repair, and microwave repair and installation. It also lists water heater, heating and cooling, and fixture work.
  • The website describes its approach as diagnose, explain the findings and the work needed, then test the appliance after the repair. Service can be booked online or by text.
  • Per its BBB profile (accessed 2026-11-21), the business started in December 2023, has been BBB accredited since August 11, 2025, and lists Israel Omotade as president. BBB showed no complaints in the last three years.

These are claims or details published by the business, not reviews or ratings from this directory.
