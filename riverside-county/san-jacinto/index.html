Independent appliance repair in San Jacinto

San Jacinto is Hemet's neighbor in the same valley, with Soboba and the foothills to the east. Fewer shops publish San Jacinto by name than list Hemet, even when they will drive both cities. The shops below name San Jacinto on a source we opened — including two Hemet-based shops and a Beaumont-based technician. If a nearby Hemet profile doesn't mention San Jacinto, still call and ask.

California registration. In California, businesses that repair major home appliances for a fee must register with the Bureau of Household Goods and Services (BHGS) as service dealers. You can look a company up in the Department of Consumer Affairs license search. If a shop published a registration number on its own website, we record that as the shop's claim — we do not invent license numbers, and you should still confirm that a registration is current before you book.
