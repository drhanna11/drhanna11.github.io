Riverside County, California

Independent appliance repair in Riverside County, California

A directory of 25 independent or locally based shops serving Riverside County — from Corona and the city of Riverside through Temecula, Hemet, and the Coachella Valley. We gathered these from the businesses' own websites or other public pages we recorded. Always call to confirm that they still serve your ZIP, and that the phone number is current.

California registration. In California, businesses that repair major home appliances for a fee must register with the Bureau of Household Goods and Services (BHGS) as service dealers. You can look a company up in the Department of Consumer Affairs license search. If a shop published a registration number on its own website, we record that as the shop's claim — we do not invent license numbers, and you should still confirm that a registration is current before you book. Search BHGS registrations. BHGS consumer guide.

Our second market: a large Inland Empire and Coachella Valley county where summer heat is hard on refrigerators, ice makers, and laundry equipment, and where California requires appliance repair dealers to register with the Bureau of Household Goods and Services.

Riverside County appliance repair questions

Do these shops cover my city?

Many shops cover several cities, and some west-county businesses also drive into San Bernardino or Orange County. Each profile lists only the service areas named on a source we opened. If your city is not listed, still call — coverage can be broader than the website copy, or it may have changed.

Are these the only independent techs in Riverside County?

No. This is a researched starting set, not a complete census of every independent in the county. Some cities, especially San Jacinto, still have few shops that published that city by name. If you run or know an independent shop that should be here, use the Get listed page.
