Independent appliance repair in Hemet

Hemet sits in the San Jacinto Valley with Valle Vista, San Jacinto, and East Hemet, inland and typically hotter than the I-15 cities. Several long-standing shops are based right here — one parts-and-repair counter dates to 1966 and an appliance showroom with its own service department to 1984 — and some Menifee- and Temecula-area technicians also drive out. This page lists every shop in the directory that names Hemet on a source we opened, Hemet-based shops first.

California registration. In California, businesses that repair major home appliances for a fee must register with the Bureau of Household Goods and Services (BHGS) as service dealers. You can look a company up in the Department of Consumer Affairs license search. If a shop published a registration number on its own website, we record that as the shop's claim — we do not invent license numbers, and you should still confirm that a registration is current before you book. Before you call a Hemet repair shop

  • Ask what the service call costs for your address before you book. Some shops price by distance: Appliance Showroom on Florida Ave publishes $76 for Hemet, San Jacinto, Valle Vista, Homeland, Nuevo, and Romoland, and higher rates for Menifee, Perris, and Beaumont ($109) or Temecula, Murrieta, Anza, and Idyllwild ($129) (accessed 2026-11-21). Elsewhere in this directory, some shops say the fee is waived if you approve the repair — check each profile. We quote only what a shop published.
  • Check the BHGS registration. California requires appliance repair dealers to register with the Bureau of Household Goods and Services. When a Hemet shop publishes a number (B & B lists License # 41773; Izzy 5 Star shows CA Lic# 51045), look it up in the DCA License Search — a number on a website is a claim, not proof.
  • Have the brand, model number, approximate age, and any extended-warranty details ready. Appliance Showroom’s service page asks callers for exactly these, and the model number lets any shop check parts before the visit.
  • Live in a mobile or manufactured home? Say so when you call. B & B Gas & Electric lists mobile home appliance repair among its services.
  • Idyllwild, Anza, and Aguanga are in the highest service-call tier on Appliance Showroom’s published rate list. If you live up the hill, ask any shop about trip charges and how far out the next opening is.

Hemet appliance repair questions

How many independent appliance repair shops in this directory serve Hemet?

7 shops in this directory publish Hemet on a source we opened: 4 based in Hemet and 3 based elsewhere that list it in their service area. This is a researched starting set, not a complete census.

Is a shop based in Hemet faster to get?

Not necessarily. Many shops route technicians across several cities, and some out-of-town shops say they have techs who live near their customers. Ask about the next available window and any trip fee when you call.
