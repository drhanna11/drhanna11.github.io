About

About Indie Appliance Repair

We are building a small, useful directory of independent appliance technicians — now in Phoenix, Arizona, and Riverside County, California — so homeowners can call a shop directly instead of entering a lead marketplace.

Where we cover

Phoenix is our first metro: a large, year-round repair market where heat stresses refrigerators and ice makers, with many documented owner-operated shops and no strong consumer directory that organizes independents by city, appliance, and brand. Riverside County is our second: the Inland Empire and Coachella Valley, where California requires appliance repair dealers to register with the Bureau of Household Goods and Services. We will keep adding verified shops and city pages in both areas, then expand to other metros one at a time.

How we research a listing

  • We open the business website (or another public page) and record the URL and the date.
  • We include only facts stated there: name, phone, website, address, hours, service area, appliances, brands, and published fees or license numbers.
  • We do not invent ratings, reviews, prices, or coverage areas. If a street address was not on the official site, we say so.
  • National chains and lead-mill landing pages are out of scope for “independent” listings. Multi-office local companies can still appear if they are local businesses rather than a national franchise; the profile will say so.

This directory currently includes 22 Phoenix-metro businesses and 25 Riverside County businesses, researched on 2026-11-18.
