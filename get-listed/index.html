For technicians and shop owners

Get listed, or ask about a featured listing

If you are an independent or locally owned appliance repair business serving a city we cover, we want accurate information on this site. Basic listings are free. Featured placements, if we offer them later, will be clearly labeled and will not bury or hide other shops.

Email us to get listed

Email us your shop details and we will verify them before publishing. We list independent and locally owned shops only — not national call centers or lead mills.

Send your request to contact@indieappliancerepair.com.

Email us to get listed

Your email app will open with the subject “Get listed: [business name]” and a short checklist in the body. Replace the bracketed text with your shop name, fill in the details, and send. A request is not a paid placement and does not guarantee a listing until we verify your information.

Please include

  • Business name
  • Phone
  • Website
  • Cities served
  • Appliances repaired
  • Whether the shop is independently owned

What we will and will not do

  • We will verify facts against a public page or a document you provide before publishing.
  • We will not invent reviews, ratings, or prices for you.
  • A featured listing, if offered, will be marked as paid or featured. It will not be the only way to appear in the directory.
  • A brand-specialist sponsorship, if offered, will be labeled as a paid sponsorship and will not be presented as a ranked “best of.”

Featured placements

Paid featured options may come later. Nothing is for sale now, and no featured or sponsored placements have been sold. If we offer them, they will stay clearly labeled.
