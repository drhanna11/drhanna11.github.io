Phoenix appliance repair questions

Do these companies cover my suburb?

Many Valley shops cover several cities. Each profile lists only the service areas named on a source we opened. If your city is not listed, still call — coverage can be broader than the website copy, or it may have changed.

Why are there no star ratings?

We do not copy or invent review scores. Ratings go stale, can be incomplete, and are easy to misuse. Use the profile to reach the shop, then check recent reviews on a platform you trust, or ask a neighbor.

Are these the only independent techs in Phoenix?

No. This is a first batch of businesses we could verify from public websites in November 2026. Licensed shops we have not yet sourced will be added over time, and shops can request a listing.
