Phoenix metro (Valley of the Sun)

Independent appliance repair in Phoenix, Arizona

A directory of 22 independent or locally based shops serving Phoenix and the Valley of the Sun. We gathered these from the businesses' own websites. Always call to confirm that they still serve your ZIP, and that the phone number is current.

Phoenix is our first metro because it is a large, year-round repair market — desert heat puts extra load on refrigerators, ice makers, and laundry equipment — and because we could document a deep bench of owner-operated and locally owned shops. City pages are rolling out one at a time, starting with Mesa; Chandler, Scottsdale, and others are next. Riverside County, California, is live as a second market.

Phoenix appliance repair questions

Do these shops cover my city?

Many Valley shops cover several cities. Each profile lists only the service areas named on a source we opened. If your city is not listed, still call — coverage can be broader than the website copy, or it may have changed.

Are these the only independent techs in Phoenix?

No. This is a researched starting set, not a complete census. If you run or know an independent shop that should be here, use the Get listed page.
