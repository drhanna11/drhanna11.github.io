From the business

  • The homepage describes Hillside Appliance Services as a family-owned business that has served the Valley for over 20 years, specializing in home refrigeration, kitchen, and laundry appliances.
  • A blog post on the site (April 7, 2025) says its technicians are factory-trained and certified by Sub-Zero for Pro 48, Integrated, and Classic models, and calls the company Scottsdale-based. That is the shop's claim; we have not confirmed it with Sub-Zero, so ask Sub-Zero for its authorized servicer list if that matters for your repair.
  • Appliances named on the site include refrigerators, freezers, ice makers, wine coolers, ovens, stoves, ranges, cooktops, microwaves, dishwashers, washers, dryers, and garbage disposals. The site also lists commercial and property-management service.
  • The site says it offers same-day service whenever possible and serves Phoenix-area cities within a 30-mile radius.
  • Published warranty: a 90-day limited labor warranty for workmanship defects; parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, typically 1 year (accessed 2026-11-22; confirm when you call).
  • The About page says the company is licensed and insured; it does not publish a license number on the pages we opened.

These are claims or details published by the business, not reviews or ratings from this directory.
