From the business

  • The Mesa page says the business is based in Mesa at 535 W. Iron Ave, Suite 130 (85210), and describes itself as a family business, not a big chain, now run by the second generation of the family.
  • The website says the company has served Arizona homes since 1976. It also does heating, cooling, and duct work, and publishes separate East Valley (480) 649-0000, Central (602) 258-1020, and West Valley (623) 931-7223 phone lines.
  • The website says the diagnostic fee is waived if you go ahead with the repair, and that repairs include a 1-year parts and labor warranty (accessed 2026-11-18; confirm when you call).
  • The Mesa page says a typical repair is about $250, quoted after the diagnostic, and that your price may be higher or lower (accessed 2026-11-18). We list this as the shop's own published figure, not a quote.
  • The Mesa page says same-day visits are usually available if you call before 3 PM, but that same-day service is not guaranteed; it gives a 3-hour arrival window and texts when the tech is on the way.
