Before you call a Chandler repair shop

  • Chandler's water is hard. The City of Chandler's 2025 drinking water quality report lists total hardness of 10.1 to 16.8 grains per gallon (173 to 288 mg/L). Scale is a common factor in dishwasher, ice maker, and refrigerator water-line problems, so mention it if you see white buildup or slow water flow.
  • Ask about the trip or diagnostic fee up front. Several shops below publish a fee policy (for example, waived if you approve the repair). We quote only what a shop published, with the date we read it.
  • Check the license claim. Arizona contractors are licensed by the Registrar of Contractors (ROC). If a shop publishes an ROC number, you can confirm it in the ROC's public contractor search before you book.
  • Sun Lakes, Chandler Heights, and the San Tan Valley edge are at the edge of some service areas. If you live out that way, ask whether there is an extra trip charge.
  • Under a manufacturer warranty? The brand may require its own authorized servicer. Call the manufacturer first, then use an independent shop for out-of-warranty work.

Chandler appliance repair questions

How many independent appliance repair shops in this directory serve Chandler?

19 shops in this directory publish Chandler on a source we opened: 3 based in Chandler and 16 based elsewhere that list it in their service area. This is a researched starting set, not a complete census.

Is a shop based in Chandler faster to get?

Not necessarily. Many shops route technicians across several cities, and some out-of-town shops say they have techs who live near their customers. Ask about the next available window and any trip fee when you call.
