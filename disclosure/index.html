Affiliate and advertising disclosure

As of 2026-11-18, this site has no active affiliate tracking links, no paid featured listings, and no sponsored posts. We do not currently earn commissions from any retailer or warranty company.

Placeholder slots

Some guide, comparison, “best for,” and city-and-appliance pages include clearly labeled “Affiliate placement” boxes. Those boxes reserve space for parts, tools, replacement-appliance, or warranty links. Until a program is approved and a tracking URL is added to our link configuration, those buttons point here — not to a merchant — and use rel="sponsored nofollow". They are not live affiliate links.

When a program is approved, we will turn on the tracking URL, keep the sponsored label, and update this page so it lists the programs actually in use.

What we may do later

Priority placements, if approved, are for higher-commission parts catalogs (for example AppliancePartsPros, RepairClinic, or Sears PartsDirect), then tools, then replacement-appliance retailers, then warranty or product-protection plans. Warranty links, if used, will stay clearly separated from the advice to call a local technician, because a warranty company may dispatch its own network.

Featured listings and sponsorships

We may later offer shops a paid, clearly labeled featured placement on a city or category page, or a labeled brand-specialist sponsorship (for example on a Sub-Zero page). Featured and sponsored listings will not be the only way to appear in the directory, and we will not invent ratings to justify a paid slot. No such placements have been sold.

Editorial standard

Listing facts come from sources recorded on each profile. We do not sell the right to hide a competitor or to publish a fake review. See About & method.
